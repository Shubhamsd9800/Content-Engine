# Content Engine

Turns raw input into published content on **Instagram (reels)** and **LinkedIn (written)** —
without a blank page, and without sounding like everyone else.

The goal is not posts. The goal is becoming a **digital asset**: a name that carries enough
trust that a cold email lands as a credible one, and that clients and opportunities
eventually arrive inbound.

---

## The niche

> **How one person builds and ships real software products — and what breaks on the way.**

AI is how the work gets done, never the identity. Tools change; the position shouldn't.

**Two audiences, two offers — this is the load-bearing decision:**

```
PEERS   students, engineers, working professionals  →  buy the PRODUCT   (deferred, post 20)
BUYERS  small business owners, solo founders         →  buy the SERVICE   (web, SaaS, MVP)
        with no technical person
```

The peers are not a leak. They are the second revenue line.

**Content is not the client channel. Outreach is. Content is the proof that makes outreach
work.**

---

## The rules that kill things

> **If a piece has no gap the source missed and nothing of yours behind it, it does not get
> published.**

> **One post, one reader.** Decided per Brief. Writing to both inside one piece is what
> makes content generic.

> **Ring 5 is never.** *"AI is changing everything."* Same hour to write, zero qualified
> attention, and it changes who shows up in the comments.

> **Every tool named declares its real stage** — found · installed · testing · shipped. A
> stage is never skipped upward. *"Use this"* requires **shipped**.

> **Content shows what he builds.** It never claims what he has delivered to clients — there
> are none yet.
