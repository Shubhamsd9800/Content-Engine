# STEP 0 — THE REFUSALS

Check these **before** anything else. Two of the five NOT-list items exist because of this
skill, and a reel is where they bite.

| Refuse outright | Why |
|---|---|
| **Day-in-my-life material.** Office routine, lunch break, friends, hanging out | NOT-list item 3. The script for this was already written once and stays unwritten |
| **Dance, trend or chamak-chalo formats.** Anything where **the format is the content** | NOT-list item 4 |
| **Someone else's story with no angle** | NOT-list item 5. The angle does not have to be his — **it has to be the reader's problem** |
| **Ring 5** — *"AI is changing everything"*, motivational content with nothing under it | same hour to shoot, zero qualified attention |

A hit is a **stop**, not a note. Send the Brief back.

# STEP 1 — THE FOUR FIELDS THAT DECIDE THE REEL

From the Brief. **Decisions already made — you do not re-decide them.**

`READER` — **peer** or **buyer**. Exactly one. If it says `mixed` or `unknown`, stop; the
Brief is not finished.

`RING` · `FUNNEL SLOT` · `STAGE`.

## READER changes the register

| | **PEER** | **BUYER** |
|---|---|---|
| **On screen** | the terminal, the diff, the error | the before and after, in plain outcomes |
| **Assume** | real tools, versions, paths | nothing. They have never heard of an MCP |
| **The unit** | the mechanism — why it broke | the consequence — what it cost |
| **Close** | what to try, or stop doing | what this means for what they're trying to get built |

**Explaining basics is not beneath this account.** Ring 2–3 is *the basics nobody is
teaching*. Shooting over a buyer's head is the failure, not the standard.

## STAGE is spoken out loud

Every tool named carries its real stage **in the script** — `found` haven't used it ·
`installed` haven't run it · `testing` partial results · `shipped` used it for real.

**Never skip a stage upward.** *"I installed it, haven't run it yet"* is a stronger line on
camera than a fake recommendation, and it is the only one he can defend.

**And never imply a client delivery.** Every ledger entry is `built-for-self`; there are
zero clients.

# STEP 2 — TWO PRODUCTION DECISIONS, STATED AT THE TOP

**FORMAT** — from the Brief's `PROOF`:
showable on screen (code, error, diff, working app, benchmark) → **screen recording** ·
a judgment, opinion, story with no artifact → **talking head** ·
strong claim *with* an artifact → **hybrid**, face for the 3-second hook then cut to screen.
**Default when unclear: screen recording.** State which and why in one line.

**LANGUAGE** — from `READER`, which is now always a single value:
**peer**, India-facing, story or rant → **Hinglish spoken, English on-screen** ·
**buyer**, international, or a technical claim → **English**.
**If Hinglish: hold a few turns of casual Hinglish with Shubham first.** Warm-up language
sets the register. Mandatory, not a nicety.

# STEP 3 — THE GATE

> **Reality must beat expectations.** Restating common knowledge is a structural loss.

Is there anything here nobody else is saying? If `GAPS` and `YOUR STANDING` are both thin,
stop and send it back. *If the points aren't unique, the video doesn't get made.*

# THE FIVE STEPS — in this order, always

## 1 · PACKAGING
The one-line idea = **the pain point of the ideal viewer**, or the rabbit hole they cannot
resist. The hook sets the expectation everything else must beat.
**Order: idea → outline → hook.** The hook is written after the outline, never before.

## 2 · OUTLINE — the uniqueness gate
Bulleted body points before any script. For each: **the what, the why, the how.** Then zoom
out: *is this unique, or regurgitation?*

**If the points are not unique, do not proceed.** For Shubham uniqueness usually comes from
**the specific real detail** — the version number, the two days lost, the thing that looked
fine and wasn't.

## 3 · INTRO — the five-part hook

| Part | Does |
|---|---|
| **1 · Immediate context** | bluntly state what this is about. **Click confirmation** — confirm the expectation the hook set |
| **2 · The common belief** | restate what they already think. They only listen once it feels about them |
| **3 · The contrarian contrast** | contradict it. This is the stun |
| **4+5 · Proof + plan** | why trust this approach, and what's coming. Either order |

**Parts 1–3 live in the first 3 seconds.** Parts 4–5 land by second 8.

### Frame broad. Land narrow.

**Open on pain or desire, never on the subject.** Some permutation of *"if you want…"* /
*"if you don't want…"*, or the cost stated flat.

> **Never open at the practitioner's level of awareness.** Most people scrolling are not
> intentionally trying to learn. A reel that opens inside the problem filters to people
> already inside it — at zero followers, almost nobody.

**4 S's in the first beat:** Subject · Stakes · Speed (under ~8 words) · Super-clear.

**The triple hook must align** — spoken, on-screen and visual say the same thing in
different words. Contradiction splits attention across three channels and the viewer leaves.

**Then consider one modifier** (`brain/psychology.md`): "in [n] seconds" · countdown ·
objection handling · beat drop on the reveal. **Never more than two.** None is the common
case.

## 4 · BODY

**Second-best point FIRST, best point second, then descending.** Two things where the second
is better creates a pattern that value is rising, so they stay for the third. Best first
makes the second a step down and trains them value is decaying.

**Value loop on every point:** context (what it is) → application (how, with examples) →
framing (why it matters, how it fits).

**Rehook at every seam.** *"That mattered. But without the next one, it doesn't hold."*

**Zoom in, zoom out.** Big picture → tactic → big picture. **Data → emotion → data.**

**Zoom into the moment, don't summarise.** Location, actions, the raw thought, the exact
words.

**Pratfall.** A failure reel from someone not yet established reads as incompetence unless
the competence travels with it. Root cause, fix and proof in the same reel — never the
failure alone, never as the punchline.

## 5 · OUTRO
Summarise, remind them of the pain solved, leave on a high note — likes, shares and rewatches
come disproportionately from the final impression. **Engineer one peak.**

**THE CTA COMES FROM THE BRIEF. Never decided here. — corrected Day 28.**

`none` is the default and it is valid and common · `question` · `link` (first comment only,
never the body). Forced CTAs are a banned pattern. This line previously hardcoded
*"conversation or return only"*, which the Brief-decides rule **replaces**. Never "DM me."

# RULES

1. **Never publish this draft.** Shubham always rewrites.
2. **One reel, one reader.** If you cannot name which, the Brief is not ready.
3. **Refuse format-led content.** If the format is the reason it exists, it does not get made.
4. **Three tracks, always separated.** Spoken ≠ on-screen text. Same meaning, different
   words. Captioning the spoken track verbatim wastes the second channel.
5. **Never open at practitioner level.** Broad hook, narrow body.
6. **Every tool named carries its real stage, spoken.** Never skip a stage upward.
7. **Never imply a client delivery.**
8. **Confidence downgrade** — authority does not transfer from the account you borrowed the
   structure from.
9. Never claim what the ledger cannot back.
10. One idea per reel.
11. **Every borrowed structure names its entry in `brain/playbook.md`** — the shape (S01-S10),
    the script devices used from §2, and any production device from §6.
    **And it names the CONFIDENCE TIER: [C] confirmed · [S] single source · [X] craft only.**
    A draft leaning on an [X] device as its spine is sent back.
12. Banned openings and words: `brain/voice.md`.
13. Log all 9 hooks in `work/log/hook-bank.md`.
