# THE TWO INPUTS THAT ARE NEVER HARDCODED

| Input | Default | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **READER** | `buyer` + `peer`, both — a profile is not a post | vocabulary · what is assumed · what the CTA asks |
| **VOICE** | `brain/voice.md` | how it sounds. What this person would never say. |

**Writing for a client:** point `VOICE` at their voice file, set `READER` from their brief.
**Nothing else changes.** Same design as `linkedin-writer` v2, and the same reason: it serves
one account today and becomes a service line later.

> **A PROFILE IS NOT A POST. The one-reader rule does not apply here.**
> `niche.md` forbids `mixed` on a Brief because a post has one opening and one reader.
> A profile has sections. **The headline is read by both. About can serve both in sequence.**
> Do not force a profile to choose the way a Brief must.

# STEP 1 — INTAKE

**Accept the profile in whatever form it arrives. All four are valid:**

```
A  the live profile, read through the Chrome extension   ← most current, preferred
B  an existing audit artifact or work/profile/*.md       ← fastest when recent
C  a LinkedIn PDF export                                  ← Resources → Save to PDF
D  pasted section text
```

**Never scrape a third-party service for his own profile.** The source that recommends it
reports it breaking on private profiles, mobile sessions and rate limits.

**Then confirm three things and do not proceed without them:**

```
☐ READER        who this profile is for. buyer · peer · both.
☐ ONE ACTION    what a visitor should do. Not "connect". A real next step.
☐ THE OFFER     what is sellable TODAY, stated plainly.
```

**If a previous audit exists, read it first and do not re-derive it.** Say what changed since.

---

# STEP 2 — GATE 1 · THE HONEST DIAGNOSTIC. No rewriting.

**Framing, and it matters more than it looks:**

> You are a friend who has hired and rejected people for ten years. You owe honesty, not
> politeness. Better to hear it here than from someone who silently passes.

**The standard "act as a recruiter" framing produces corporate politeness.** This one produces
an actual markup. Use it.

**Run the BUZZWORD SCAN first, before any score.** Both lists — the source list in
`linkedin-profile-sources.md` PART 2 **and** `voice.md` HARD RULES. They overlap and neither
contains the other. **Every flag gets a specific replacement, not a note in passing.**

**Then answer five questions, quoting his actual lines back:**

```
1  IDENTITY GAP     One sentence: what identity does the profile project today?
                    One sentence: what identity does the stated offer need?
                    Name the mismatch.
2  KEYWORD GAP      Which terms would a buyer type that the profile does not contain?
3  CREDIBILITY GAP  Which real artifacts in ledger.md does the profile bury or omit?
4  SEARCH           Would this profile appear for the three most likely searches? Why not?
5  THE ONE LINE     If the profile had to win on one sentence — what does it say today,
                    what should it say?
```

**SCORE each section 1–10, one sentence of diagnosis each.**

```
1-3   actively working against the goal — confusing, misleading, or missing
4-6   neutral. present but forgettable. will not convert.
7-8   strong. clear and functional. minor sharpening.
9-10  exceptional. Do not award without genuinely remarkable copy.
```

**Score what is actually there, not what you wish were there.** A profile that scores 3 is told
it scores 3, with a roadmap — never softened into "great foundation".

**GATE: show the diagnostic and STOP.** Do not rewrite in the same turn.

---

# STEP 3 — SEQUENCE, NEVER STACK

> **THE RULE THIS SKILL IS BUILT ON.** Stacking six jobs into one ask produces a shallow
> rewrite of every section instead of a strong rewrite of the one that matters.
> **One section per pass. Show it. Wait. Then the next.**

**Default order — highest leverage first, not top-to-bottom:**

```
1  ABOUT        if missing or weak. The only place a story and a CTA can live.
2  HEADLINE     seen in every search result and every comment.
3  FEATURED     usually the cheapest real gain on the page.
4  EXPERIENCE   what a serious visitor actually reads.
5  SKILLS       three pinned slots decide who finds him.
6  AI VISIBILITY the pass nobody else runs.
7  BANNER + PHOTO  visual, and last on purpose.
```

**Re-order if the diagnostic says so. Say why.**

---

## 3A · ABOUT

```
HOOK         1-2 sentences. Everything after line 2 is behind "see more" on mobile.
             NEVER "I'm a [title]". Lead with the claim, not the person.
CREDIBILITY  2-4 sentences. What kinds of problems, what contexts. Not "X years".
PROOF        2-4 sentences. Real artifacts from ledger.md. Numbers where they exist.
CTA          1-2 sentences. ONE next step, matched to the stated action.
             Not "feel free to reach out."
```

**Give three alternative opening hooks to test.** Nobody knows which lands until it runs.

**Length: the sources conflict — 220 words against roughly 330. Do not pick a number.**
**The hook is the variable, not the length.** Write what the idea needs and say so.

**Line breaks every one to two sentences. This is functional, not decorative** — most views are
mobile and a wall of text is scrolled past.

---

## 3B · HEADLINE

**THE REAL CONSTRAINT, WHICH NO SOURCE STATES:** the LinkedIn maximum is 220 characters, but
**mobile truncates at roughly 45.** Everything after that is behind a tap almost nobody takes.

```
FRONT-LOAD THE FIRST 45 CHARACTERS. Everything else is a bonus.
```

**Do not spend those characters on anything the page already displays** — LinkedIn shows the
current employer beside the name automatically, with a logo. Repeating it buys it twice.

> **THE EMPLOYER IS NOT BANNED FROM THE PROFILE. Decided Day 29.**
> `niche.md` NOT-list item 1 governs **posts**, where he speaks in his own voice.
> **A profile is a factual employment record. Capgemini stays in EXPERIENCE, stated plainly.**
> The headline argument is about character economy, never concealment. Do not re-raise this.

**Write three variants, each a different strategy:**

```
A  AUTHORITY-FORWARD  [what he builds] who [specific outcome for a specific audience]
B  OUTCOME-FORWARD    lead with the result. identity second.
C  NICHE-SPECIFIC     audience + method + outcome, in a way no competitor can copy.
```

**Every variant must contain at least one searchable keyword** and must not be a job title
alone. Then name which to test first, who it will and will not attract, and what to watch over
30 days.

---

## 3C · FEATURED

Score each existing item **1-10 on trust · relevance to the stated action · click likelihood.**
Keep 3-6. **Strongest first — most visitors will not scroll.**
For every gap, **name the one specific asset to create.** Not "add more" — the actual piece.
Give each slot the one-line description that earns a mobile tap.

---

## 3D · EXPERIENCE

```
[Strong verb] + [scope or scale] + [measurable outcome] + [impact]

· 3 bullets per role MAX
· achievement-first, never "responsible for"
· 15 words per bullet
· lead with the most impressive, not the most chronological
· show BEFORE and AFTER side by side
· banned verbs: spearheaded · championed · drove alignment · leveraged synergies ·
  owned the vision · delivered on · responsible for · tasked with · helped with
```

**`[METRIC NEEDED]` — never invent a number.** Flag the exact place one belongs so he can go
and find it. **A flagged gap is a successful output.** Approximate stays approximate: *"about
two days"* beats a fabricated *"16 hours"*, and a real number beats both.

**Flag any bullet that still reads like a job description after the rewrite.**

---

## 3E · SKILLS

**LinkedIn pins exactly THREE and those carry the most algorithmic weight.** Two sources agree
independently. Recruiters and buyers filter search by skill — the wrong three make him
invisible to the right people.

```
· name the three to pin, and why those three
· flag anything generic — Microsoft Office, Teamwork, Communication
· flag any skill relevant to the offer that is MISSING
· flag any skill actively pulling away from the positioning
```

---

## 3F · AI VISIBILITY

**The pass no other profile tool runs.** Full checklist in
`linkedin-profile-sources.md` PART 4. Score ✅ / ⚠️ / ❌ across all eight, then give the three
most actionable moves.

> **CHECK 3 — third-party mentions — CANNOT BE PASSED AND MUST NEVER BE FAKED.**
> No media, no named clients, no investor logos. **Substitute the ARTIFACT HOOK:** a public
> repo, a documented system, a real build ARE external and checkable. They are not press
> mentions and must not be dressed as them.

**Check 4 is the one he can win cheaply:** the profile's vocabulary must match the vocabulary in
his posts. If the profile says one thing and the posts say another, a model treats them as two
different people.

---

# STEP 4 — GATE 2 · THE RULES THAT KILL A DRAFT

**Run after every section, before showing it.**

```
☐ Every buzzword flagged AND replaced with something specific
☐ No invented metric, client, date or result — flagged instead
☐ ARTIFACT HOOK, never credential hook. No tenure doing proof's job.
☐ OFFER vs CLAIM:
     ✓ "I build LinkedIn content systems for founders"   an offer. legitimate.
     ✗ "I've grown founders' accounts"                   a claim. zero clients. NOT-list.
☐ Nothing implies a client delivery. BUILT, NOT DELIVERED.
☐ Every tool named carries its real stage: found · installed · testing · shipped.
     Sellable today: web development, SaaS and MVP builds.
     Testing, NOT shipped: copywriting and ghostwriting as a service.
☐ No money number. No employer in the headline. No politics, no religion.
☐ Would this sentence appear unchanged on ten thousand other profiles? Then rewrite it.
```

**Read it aloud.** Stumble → revise. Sounds like something he would never say → revise.

---

# STEP 5 — CLOSE

**End every run with the 24-hour rule, in these words or close to them:**

> **Push it live within 24 hours.** You can iterate on a live profile. You cannot iterate on a
> draft sitting in a chat. Watch two weeks of real signal, then refine.

**Then write the result to `work/profile/linkedin.md`** — the live copy, section by section,
dated, with what changed and what is still open.

# RULES

```
 1  SEQUENCE, NEVER STACK. One section per pass. This is the skill's whole premise.
 2  DIAGNOSTIC BEFORE REWRITING. Always. Gate 1 is not optional.
 3  NEVER INVENT a metric, client, credential, date or result. Flag it.
 4  ARTIFACT HOOK, never credential hook. Authority comes from what he BUILT.
 5  OFFER vs CLAIM. An offer is legitimate. A track record he does not have is not.
 6  ledger.md IS THE ANCHOR. No job descriptions — he is not job hunting.
 7  THE EMPLOYER STAYS IN EXPERIENCE. Settled Day 29. Do not re-raise it.
 8  FRONT-LOAD 45 CHARACTERS in the headline. The 220 limit is not a target.
 9  SCORE HONESTLY. A 3 is told it is a 3, with a roadmap. Never "great foundation".
10  READER and VOICE are inputs. Never hardcode them — this must point at a client later.
11  NEVER WRITE A POST HERE. That is linkedin-writer. Suggesting post topics is fine.
12  A profile is not a Brief. `mixed` READER is legal here and only here.
```
