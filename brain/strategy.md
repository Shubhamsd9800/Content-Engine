# strategy.md — positioning, force and the funnel

`psychology.md` is about the reader's brain. `niche.md` is who the work is for and what it
is about. **This file is about where the whole effort points** — read it before any decision
about platform, cadence, or what to refuse.

---

# STRATEGY IS POSITIONING. TACTICS ARE EXECUTION.

> A better hook is a tactic. Being the only person in a category is strategy.
> **No amount of tactics rescues bad positioning.**

The position is settled and lives in `brain/niche.md`:

> **How one person builds and ships real software products — and what breaks on the way.**

AI is how the work gets done, never the identity. Tools change; the position shouldn't.

**The behaviour, not a title:** *I go a step ahead into AI and building, and bring back the
part that actually works.* A title is a claim. A behaviour is proved by the next post — and
it survives being wrong, because reporting that a tool disappointed him **is** the position.

# TWO AUDIENCES, TWO OFFERS

This is the load-bearing decision in the whole engine.

```
PEERS   students, engineers, working professionals   →  buy the PRODUCT
BUYERS  small business owners, solo founders          →  buy the SERVICE
        with no technical person
```

**The peers are not a leak. They are the second revenue line.**

Most creators run one or the other. Tooling creators reach peers and sell products; outcome
creators reach buyers and sell services. Running both here is deliberate.

**What follows from it:**

- **One post, one reader.** Decided per Brief when the idea arrives, never by a standing
  rule. Writing to both inside one piece is what makes content generic.
- **The ratio is watched, never assumed.** Every Brief records `READER`. At twenty posts
  the real split is a fact instead of a guess.
- **The product offer waits.** There is no product yet and zero posts. A `product` Brief is
  a hold until post 20. BOFU to a peer with nothing to sell is a promise the engine can't
  keep.
- **Roughly 60–70% of output at the 3-2-2 mix reaches peers.** That is expected, not a
  failure. It is the shape the two-offer model produces.

# HOW CLIENTS ACTUALLY ARRIVE

> **Content is not the client channel. Outreach is.**
> **Content is the proof that makes outreach work.**

Outreach starts **September** — cold email, direct conversations. When one of those lands,
the recipient searches the name. What they find is the difference between a cold email and
a credible one.

| Lever | What | Speed | Ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Content** | the public body of work | slow, compounds forever | very high |
| **Direct outreach** | email, DMs, replies, targeted conversations | fast, does not compound | low |

**At zero followers, content is writing for an empty room.** Still correct — it is the asset
— but the asset takes months. Outreach produces the first clients *while* the asset is
small. Ignoring the second lever is the commonest way a personal brand takes two years to do
what could take four.

**No `outreach` skill exists yet.** It is the most valuable missing skill in the folder.

# CONCENTRATION OF FORCE

> Do nothing that is useless.

At zero followers the force is small, so it does not get divided:

- **One idea per piece.** Three ideas is force divided by three.
- **One trust leg per post.** One reader per post.
- **Text first, reel as the multiplier.** The expensive production step only runs on ideas
  already proven in writing.
- **3–5 reference creators.** More produces overlap, not insight.
- **Platform.** Currently LinkedIn and Instagram together. **This is not settled** — Koe:
  *"You'd gain more followers by focusing on one platform first, which can then be used as
  leverage to grow on the other."* Open decision, recorded in `niche.md`.

Every "should we also…" gets tested against this section first.

# THE BULLSEYE — the actual rings

Not a scaffold. These are the rings, filled.

| Ring | Content | Per seven |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | resources found and where · what I built and what broke · systems I build and how they're wired · getting AI to produce work that doesn't look AI-generated | **3** |
| **2–3** | the basics nobody is teaching · beliefs I can argue with evidence · what a business needs built and what getting it wrong costs | **2** |
| **4** | AI and industry news with my opinion attached · founders' journeys told from the reader's current position | **2** |
| **5** | *"AI is changing everything."* Motivational quotes with nothing under them. | **0 — never** |

**Ring 5 costs the same hour to write, brings zero qualified attention, and changes who
shows up in the comments.** That last part is why it is a hard never and not a preference.

**A tradeoff recorded on purpose:** resource posts sit at ring 1 by decision. They are the
most copyable content on the internet and the fastest route to being found. **The building
posts are the moat; the resource posts are the reach.** Revisit at 20 posts with real
numbers.

**`topic-scout` naturally supplies ring 4** — trending, outlier-driven material. Ring 4 is
**two of seven, the smallest slice.** An obedient daily sweep will over-supply it. Ring 1
comes from what he actually did and found, not from a sweep.

# THE FUNNEL — every post declares a slot

> **Every post has a goal, or you don't post it.**

A gate, not a suggestion. It kills the commonest failure in personal branding: posting
because it is Tuesday.

| Slot | Day | Job | Trust leg |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TOFU** | Mon | make new people find you — strong opinions, a release you have a view on, **free value resources** | attraction |
| **MOFU** | Wed | make the people who found you believe you — lessons, **mistakes**, tutorials, a real build | authenticity + authority |
| **BOFU** | Fri | say what you build and who it is for | the offer |

**The debt structure, and it is the point:** the earlier slots **earn the right** to ask in
the later one. You do not get Friday unless Monday and Wednesday happened.

**BOFU splits by reader.** To a **buyer**: state plainly what you build and who for — not a
sale, a statement. To a **peer**: there is nothing to sell yet, so a peer-BOFU is a hold
until the product exists.

**The TOFU format that works hardest:** a free resource that costs you a day and saves a
stranger a day. The value has to be real — that is why it travels. Not a lead-magnet trick.

# HOOK FRAMING — broad opening, narrow subject

From Koe, and it overrides the instinct to open at your own level of awareness:

> *"Most people on social media aren't intentionally trying to learn."*
> *"You need to frame your ideas as broad and desirable."*

**Open on pain or desire.** Some permutation of *"if you want…"* / *"if you don't want…"*.

**Never open at the practitioner's level.** *"My chunk sizes were perfect, my retrieval was
garbage"* is a practitioner opening — it filters to people already inside the problem, which
at zero followers is almost nobody.

**Broad hook. Narrow subject.** The hook earns the read; the body is as specific as it
needs to be.

# PERSONAL BRAND IS A TRAFFIC SOURCE, NOT A BUSINESS

The brand generates qualified attention. A **separate offer** converts it — service to
buyers, product to peers.

Practical consequence: do not judge the engine by whether posts feel like selling. They are
not supposed to. They make the right people trust him enough to be reachable when the offer
appears — and credible when the outreach email lands.

# PROOF BEATS CLAIMS

~1.5 years professional. **Zero independent clients.** Borrowing the posture of someone ten
years in is detected instantly.

The edge is **learning in public with real artifacts** — working code, real systems, real
mistakes stated plainly. Not a weaker position than expertise, a *different* one, and at
this stage the only honest one.

**The line that must not blur:**

> Content shows what he **builds.** Content never claims what he has **delivered to
> clients** — not until one exists.

Every ledger entry today is `built-for-self`. A real ledger entry can still be used to imply
something false; that is a separate check, and it lives on the Brief as `STANDING TYPE`.

**Priority order for what gets sold:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **P1** | Web development — capability proven daily, no independent client yet |
| **P2** | LinkedIn copywriting — **testing**, on his own account first |
| **P3** | SaaS / MVP builds — capability proven, no client yet |
| — | AI automations, workflow systems — learning-stage. Content may explore. Content may not claim. |
