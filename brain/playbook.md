## HOW TO USE IT

```
you have an idea
   │
   ├─ 1  brief-builder assigns  RING · READER · PLATFORM · SLOT      → niche.md
   ├─ 2  WHICH SHAPE fits it?                                        → §1
   ├─ 3  HOW DO THE WORDS GO inside that shape?                      → §2
   ├─ 4  WHAT IS THE FIRST LINE?                                     → §3
   ├─ 5  WHAT JOB IS THE CAPTION DOING?                              → §4
   ├─ 6  IS THIS LINKEDIN?  → stop. brain/linkedin-playbook.md, not this file.
   ├─ 7  HOW DO I SHOOT IT?                                          → §6
   ├─ 8  WHAT MUST HOLD?                                             → §7
   └─ 9  WHAT DO I NOT DO?                                           → §8
```

**One idea per piece. One reader per piece.** Both are `niche.md` and neither is negotiable.

> **Shapes are reusable forever. Topics are theirs.**
> *"Opens on a stated outcome in six words, no context, then undercuts it"* is a shape.
> *"Talks about RAG chunking"* is a topic. **Taking topics is how you become a worse copy of
> someone with a bigger account.**

> **THE CONFIDENCE DOWNGRADE.** A structure from a large account carries that account's
> authority, and **authority does not transfer.** Every entry records what it sounds like at
> your actual standing — 1.5 years in, real artifacts, no follower base, zero clients.

**⚠️ THE ANGLE RULE — `niche.md` NOT-LIST item 5 governs this shape.**

> **THE BAR IS LOW AND IT IS ONLY THIS:** the story must be told **FOR** the reader, not
> **ABOUT** the subject. Something in it must be theirs to keep.
>
> **WHAT FAILS — the whole of it:** *"Physics Wallah struggled and it's inspiring."*
> No angle at all. **Admiration is not a takeaway.** It fails for having NOTHING, not for
> missing one particular device.
>
> **WHAT PASSES — any ONE of these is enough:**
> · a decision the reader also faces · a belief the story corrects · a mechanism they can
> reuse · a named cost (*"two years, no salary"*) · a stage they recognise ← **one option,
> NOT the rule**
>
> **AND THE HOOK IS NOT THE ANGLE. Do not open on the reader.** Her best post opens on an
> impossible fact; the reader-relevance arrives at the **close**.
> **OPEN ON THE STORY. LAND ON THE READER.** Opening on the reader is a lecture.
>
> **KEEP IT SHORT.** The angle is a sentence, not a framework. If it needs explaining, it is
> not an angle.

> ⭐ **ADDED DAY 27 — SHE IS THE ONLY REHOOK SOURCE IN THE PROJECT.** Seven devices across

            20s — the list is visibly finite; the end is always in sight
REWARD      a menu, not a method. They leave knowing WHAT, rarely HOW.
MECHANIC    pattern completion + SELF-SELECTION. Not the orienting response — the opposite.

> **THIS IS NOT A STEP LIST AND THE DIFFERENCE DECIDES THE RETENTION CURVE.**
> A **step list** retains by promising **completion** — do 1, then 2, then 3, all for the same
> reader. **It fails if a step is missing.**
> A **lottery list** retains by **self-selection** — each line is another chance that this one
> is about you. **It fails if the items are too similar to each other.**

**THREE MOVES THAT COME WITH IT:**
- **STATE THE UNSHOWN REMAINDER [C] 2** — *"…along with 9 more in the DM"* · *"there are 200
  more of these."* `[kushal #12]` `[theautomationguy #5]`
- **CONCEDE ONE ITEM [X]** — *"you probably already know this one."* Costs one item, buys
  credibility for the rest. `[devtalks #12]`
- **THE ONE-LINE VERSION [S]** — a single qualifying question instead of N doors.
  *"Do you want better front-end designs with Claude Code?"* `[nick_saraev #10]`

⚠️ **BREAK THE FRAME ONCE, on the highest-stakes item.** Five identical sentences make the
sixth variation land hard. `[kushal #12 — "…then this project has to be in your RESUME"]`
⚠️ **AND NAME THE THING.** `kushal #12` says *"this project"* six times and never names one —

## 2.3 · THE BODY

**Order.** Kallaway: **second-best point first, best point second.** Two things escalating
trains the reader that value is climbing. **Three genuine exceptions:**
- **a pipeline** — causality fixes the order, and it escalates for free `[shashwat #11]`
- **a narrative** — a story cannot put its reversal first `[roshni #11]`
- **a lottery list** — there is no "best", only "yours"

**The value loop, per point:** `what` it is → `how` to do it → `why` it matters.

> ### 🔴 A 90-WORD SCRIPT HAS ROOM FOR `how` OR `why`, NOT BOTH. PICK ONE.
>
> | account | `how` | `why` | GAP (likes) |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | `developer_mannjadwani` | **14/14** | 1/14 | 21.74× |
> | `forseth.ai` | 2/12 | **12/12** | 35.99× |
>
> **Both extremes win. What decides which you can run is AUTHORITY.** `forseth.ai` argues
> because his artifact proves itself continuously. **`mannjadwani` cannot argue — no visible
> standing — so he instructs.**
>
> **CONSEQUENCE, and it is a real recommendation:** at 1.5 years with zero followers, **the
> `how` half is available today and the `why` half is not.** You can show exactly how to do
> something. You cannot yet claim why it matters and be believed. **The `why` unlocks once
> there is a visible artifact — the `forseth.ai` sequence: build the thing that proves itself,
> then earn the right to argue.**

**THE CONSEQUENCE CHAIN · [C] 4 accounts — the most portable device in this file.**
Never stop at what the thing is. Chain it, and land on the reader's outcome:
```
"use this music → your CLIENT'S video goes viral → the client hires you again"   [devtalks #8]
"stop testing editors → be the creative director → plug into Claude → done"       [forseth #6]
"…or build the wrong thing for THREE WEEKS — you can cancel before starting"      [roshni #4]
```
- **It can land on a loss AVOIDED, not only a gain won.** *"Three weeks"* is the number doing
  the work — specific, small, already lost by the reader.
- **THE FAILURE-AVOIDED CLAUSE [C]** — the miniature, once per list item, six words each:
  *"…so your UI stops looking vibe-coded"*, *"…before you ship anything"*, *"no more finding
  out during a client demo."* **The cheapest `why` device found.** `[nick_saraev]`
- **One chain at 0:00 can carry the `why` for an entire body.** `[devtalks #8]`

**THE LEVER NOBODY KNEW ABOUT · [C] 2** — the fact that explains why the thing is **possible at
all**. Hold it for the midpoint. Signpost it.
```
"Most Indian roads are still under construction warranty — so the contractor
 who built it is actually responsible."      → a complaint becomes a claim   [vaibhav #11]
"Here's the part nobody's talking about — Apache 2.0. Build on this and sell it.
 Zero royalty."                              → a toy becomes a business      [thevibefounder X]
```
**Not a feature of the thing — the reason it can exist.** For a developer audience the licence
line is the most commercially useful sentence in this file, and it is free to say about any
permissive repo.

**THE NUMBER STACK [X]** — figures ordered by escalating impossibility, no connectives, ending
on the smallest and most absurd. *"8GB of RAM — 2.78 trillion parameters, one CPU, zero graphics
cards, and the whole engine is 176 kilobytes."* **The stack is the argument; explain none of
them.** For a technical reader, **end on correctness, not speed** — *"…and it came out bit-exact
against a full-size run."* `[thevibefounder U2/U3]`

**THE CONSTRAINT THAT UNLOCKS [X]** — never state a spec alone. State it through what it makes
possible. *"It reads your SCREEN, not a URL — so it takes pages behind a login, dashboards, the
inside of a paid product."* `[thevibefounder U1]`

**THE ACCESSIBILITY NUMBER [S]** — for a technical audience, lead on the **requirement**, not
the feature. *"Runs on 8GB."* *"One command."* *"No API key."* `[nick_saraev #9]`

**H14 · THE PHASED WALKTHROUGH [S]** — *"Phase one… Phase two… Phase three."* **The phase
number is a progress bar. The cheapest retention device that exists.** `[jason #8]`

> **The Day 26 conflict is resolved — and not by averaging.** It was never about length.
> **It is about what job the caption is doing, and that is set by the FORMAT.**
> When the audio carries the content, the caption is a title. **When there is no audio — a
> carousel, a LinkedIn post — the caption IS the piece.**
> `[socialmasla — 8-word title on a reel, 65-word argument on a carousel, same account]`

**THE SERIES INDEX · [C] 4 accounts.** *"Day 4 of letting Claude Code edit every video I post"*
· *"Ep 16: Building your freelance business"*. **The card carries the content; the caption
carries the shelf.** An open loop at the SERIES level — "Ep 16" implies fifteen behind it and
one coming, and the loop never closes by design.
- ⭐ **A DAY COUNT IS A PUBLIC EXPERIMENT WITH AN UNKNOWN ENDING.** *"Day 4 of letting Claude
  edit every video"* is not selling a course — it invites the reader to watch whether it works.
  **`niche.md`'s anti-vision and post-mortem both point at exactly this, and it costs nothing.**
- ⚠️ **A series is not judged by its first episodes.** `[shashwat's Ep1 at 0.17×]` vs
  `[devtalks Ep16 — his best non-lottery post]`. **What kills a series is stopping at Ep 3
  because Ep 1 did 0.17×.** Take the index; **leave the multiplier** — *"building you 100x"* is
  a promise nothing substantiates.

**SPLIT THE STORY AND THE THESIS ACROSS THE TWO CHANNELS [S].** The reel tells the story; the
caption makes the argument. Neither repeats the other. `[roshni #11 — the script never says
"the problem isn't India's talent"; the caption does]`

**THE AUDIO SELLS, THE CAPTION SUBSTANTIATES [S].** The audio rounds up and names a rival; the
caption qualifies, cites the source, gives real numbers. `[nick_saraev — 3 of 3]`
⚠️ **Take the split. Do NOT take the drift** — his audio says 80,000 stars, his caption says
76,000, in one post. **An engineering audience catches that instantly.**

### THE THREE RULES OF THE KIT

**1 · THE TEXT IS NOT A SUBTITLE.** If the on-screen text repeats the spoken words, one channel
is wasted. **Spoken = the sentence. Text = the payoff word from it, enlarged.**

**2 · THE VISUAL MUST ALREADY BE DOING THE THING.** A face talking about a dashboard is one
channel. A dashboard running while a face talks about it is two. **Never open on a face alone.**

**3 · MIXED WEIGHT, NOT MIXED COLOUR.** One accent colour and 3–4× size jumps:
*"if / this / feels like / **AI SLOP**"*. **Size carries the emphasis. Colour carries the
brand.** Two colours in one hook reads as a template.

### R-B · THE SENTENCE MUST RUN FROM YOU TO THE READER · **AMENDED DAY 27**
**OLD:** *"the number must be theirs, not yours."* Rested on one example — `[mannjadwani #6]`,

> | *"Instead of wasting your tokens, do this"* | you → yourself *(a warning)* | **0.26×** |

### R-C · THE GATE IS A CONVERSION MECHANIC, NOT A DISTRIBUTION ONE · **AMENDED DAY 27**
