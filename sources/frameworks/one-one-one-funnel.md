## The gate

> **Every post should have a goal. If it doesn't have a goal, you shouldn't post it.**

A gate, not a suggestion. It kills the commonest failure in personal branding — posting
because it is Tuesday.

## The debt structure

> With the earlier post types you **earn the right** to ask people for something.

You do not get to ask until you have attracted and earned trust. The order is not a
preference, it is a debt.

## The three slots

| Slot | Job | Formats |
|---|---|---|
| **TOFU** · Monday | make new people find you | hot takes · strong opinions about the industry · story posts · a release you have a view on · *"something I saw / read / thought"* · **free value resources** |
| **MOFU** · Wednesday | make the people who found you believe you | lessons · **mistakes** · tutorials · educational video · past work · projects · client pain points · day in the life |
| **BOFU** · Friday | say what you do and where to go | what you build · who it's for · testimonial + CTA · the honest ask |

## The evidence

Her strongest post was TOFU free-value: 6–8 hours collecting 50 LinkedIn banner examples,
given away → **3,800 comments, 135,000 impressions.**

The extractable mechanism: **a free resource that costs you a day and saves a stranger a
day is the highest-leverage TOFU format there is.** Not a lead-magnet trick — the value is
real, which is why it travels.

## Why it matters here

**This is the cadence for the Trust Matrix.**

| Trust Matrix | Slot |
|---|---|
| Attraction | TOFU — Monday |
| Authenticity + Authority | MOFU — Wednesday |
| *(the offer, which is separate from the brand)* | BOFU — Friday |

We had three legs and no schedule for hitting them.

**It also forces what would otherwise be missing.** Left alone, a content plan drifts
entirely into MOFU — lessons and stories and behind-the-scenes — and never reaches a new
person or makes an ask. The funnel forces the other two.

## Conflicts

**vs the CTA rule at zero followers.** Ours: the CTA drives conversation or return, never
"DM me." Her BOFU slot asks directly. **Resolution: keep the Friday slot; at zero followers
BOFU means stating plainly what you build and who it is for.** The direct ask unlocks with
an audience.

**Agrees with Koe** that content and the offer are different things.
