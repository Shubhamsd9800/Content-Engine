1. A rejected hook often fits a different post exactly.
2. Patterns in which hooks he keeps choosing are real `brain/voice.md` data.
3. Reading ten old hooks before writing beats a blank page.

contrarian contrast, stated outcome, the undercut, objection handling. Not "it's punchy."
