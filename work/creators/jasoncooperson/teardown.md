### J1 · THE BORROWED AUTHORITY OPEN — 4 of 12 · **WATCH**, and read the warning

Both of his top two posts open by handing the credibility to somebody else, then positioning
himself as the person who did the work of packaging it.

```
0-3s   A NAMED INSTITUTION + A NUMBER.
       "A new study from Stanford just came out that proves Claude agrees with you
        49% more than a human would."
3-8s   THE CONSEQUENCE, IN THEIR WORDS.
       "…it's basically just gonna be the world's greatest yes man. Even when your
        idea is dumb as—"          ← cuts himself off. deliberate.
8-12s  THE PROMISE, BOUNDED.
       "Here's how to fix this with one single prompt."
12-…   A SECOND BORROWED NAME, bigger than the first.
       "The fix comes from the GOAT, Andrej Karpathy — founding member at OpenAI,
        who recently left to work for Anthropic."
       + THE LABOUR CLAIM: "I already ripped his GitHub repo. I took a look at all
        the files and packaged it up into a nice little skill."
close  Comment keyword + "the skill I took hours to make, for completely free."
```

**Stanford. Karpathy. OpenAI. Anthropic. Google. Vercel.** He never asks you to trust him — he
asks you to trust the names, and positions himself as the one who did the boring part.

**MECHANIC** — authority transfer plus **the labour claim**: *"I took hours to make this and I'm
giving it away."* Reciprocity with a stated price.
**RETENTION** — **5s:** an unfamiliar number about a tool you use daily. **12s:** a second, bigger
name arrives — the value is *rising*, which is `psychology.md`'s second-best-point-first rule
applied to credibility rather than to content. **20s:** the five agents are listed one at a time,
each a small complete payoff.
**REWARD** — a named, runnable thing (`/llm-council`) plus a reason it exists.
**AWARENESS** — **practitioner-level, and this is the problem.** *"Claude agrees with you 49% more
than a human"* means nothing to someone who does not use Claude daily.
**READER** — peer, despite the buyer column.

> **DOWNGRADE — the sharpest one found so far.** *"I ripped his GitHub repo and packaged it into
> a skill"* is a **posture that requires an audience already assuming competence.** At 1.5 years
> with no following, the same sentence reads as *I copied someone's repo*. `psychology.md`'s
> confidence-downgrade line is exact: **"Here's why everyone is wrong about X" → "Here's what
> broke when I tried X."**
>
> **The adapted version:** keep the borrowed name and the packaging labour; **drop the ownership
> tone and add the failure.** *"Karpathy's LLM Council is a repo, not a tool. I spent an evening
> turning it into something I could actually run — here's the part that broke twice."* Same
> value, no borrowed posture, and the mistake buys the credibility the follower count cannot.

**TRANSFERS — partly.** 4 of 12 is below the bar anyway. **WATCH.**

## ── PACING ──

Screen recording dominant, face in a corner. Dense — he narrates a working system while it runs.
No dead air, no music-only beats, no scenes. **Information continuously, which `psychology.md`
warns against**: *an audience fed pure data settles onto one flat emotional level and leaves.*

**He gets away with it because the screen is doing the emotional work** — the thing is visibly
running. Take away the screen recording and this pacing dies.
