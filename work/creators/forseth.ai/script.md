## ── THE SPINE — 3 of 3, and it is one sentence ──

```
Every winner opens on the SAME CLAIM, about the artifact the viewer is currently inside:

  #6  "This entire video was edited by Claude Code."
  #7  "This entire video was edited by Claude Code. The captions, the cuts, the motion
       graphics, every single thing you see."
  #5  "This entire video was completely edited by Claude Code. I didn't put it there.
       Claude actually did."
```

> **The proof and the piece are the same object.** He does not describe a tool, show a demo, or
> cut to a screen recording. **The evidence is the thing being watched.** Every second the
> viewer keeps watching is another second of the claim proving itself.
>
> **This is `S01 · THE SELF-DEMONSTRATING ARTIFACT`, and this is why it is his.** But the
> teardown recorded the *shape*. What it missed is that the shape has a **script consequence**:
> because the proof is running continuously, **he never has to spend a beat on proof.**
> All three intros skip the proof beat, and lose nothing. **The format pays for it.**

## ── SCRIPT #7 · 13.85x / 14.32x · ~200 words ── ⭐ **the most complete script in fifteen**

```
PACKAGING      caption: "comment 'edit' and i'll send the breakdown over👇
                        Day 4 of letting claude code edit every video I post"
               → TWO LINES, TWO JOBS: line 1 is the gate, line 2 is THE SHELF.

               → **SPECIFICITY AS THE BEAT**, same mechanic as `shashwat___agarwal` #11.
                 Don't restate the claim — enumerate it.

INTRO          context        ✅  line 1
               common belief  ✅✅ **PERFORMED, NOT STATED** — the second instance of this
                                 device in fifteen scripts, and the other is
                                 `projectonepercent01` #3:
                                   "You know the type of AI slop that I mean. Purple,
                                    gradient, ugly stuff. Everything is just so overused."
                                 → He does not say "people think AI video looks bad." He
                                   makes the viewer picture it, in three fragments, and
                                   lands on the word they already use: **slop**.
               contrarian     ✅  "Here's my prediction… in a couple of months your whole
                                  entire feed will look like this. **And it's not the tool.
                                  It's just they don't know how to use it properly.**"
                                 → The contrarian is not "AI video is good." It is
                                   **"you are blaming the wrong thing."** Reassigning blame
                                   is a stronger turn than reversing a verdict.
               proof          ✗  SKIPPED — the format is the proof. See THE SPINE.
               plan           ✗  SKIPPED — no list, no count. This script has no body list
                                 at all; it is an ARGUMENT, not an enumeration.

               → THREE OF FIVE, and the two skipped are skipped for structural reasons.

               ⚠️ AND ONE BEAT KALLAWAY DOES NOT HAVE, PLACED BEFORE ALL OF THEM:
                 "So if this feels like AI slop, I've probably shot myself in the foot."
               → **NAME IT: THE PRE-EMPTIVE OBJECTION.** Sentence two, before any argument,
                 he states the criticism the viewer is forming — and stakes himself on it.
                 `psychology.md` calls this objection handling; nothing in `swipe.md` puts it
                 in POSITION TWO. **Naming the doubt before it hardens is what buys the next
                 fifteen seconds.**

BODY           NOT A LIST. Four argument beats:
                 1  the prediction (feeds will fill with this)
                 2  the reassignment (it's not the tool, it's the guidelines)
                 3  the diagnosis — "the problem is people post the first thing it spits out"
                 4  the resolution — "this video came from the same tool that makes the
                    sloppy ones, but with the correct guidelines, the correct skill"
               verdict: **ESCALATING**, and beat 4 closes the loop opened by the
               pre-emptive objection in sentence two. **The only script in fifteen with a
               loop that opens early and genuinely closes.**

VALUE LOOP     what 4/4 · how 1/4 · why 4/4
               → **why 4/4 — the highest in fifteen.** Every beat is framing. Nothing here is
                 a resource; it is all reasoning. **This is what a script with no list looks
                 like, and it is his second-best post.**

REHOOKS        ✅✅ **THE BEST DEVICE FOUND IN FIFTEEN SCRIPTS:**
                 "So that's exactly why you're still watching."
               → **NAME IT: THE PROOF IS YOU.** He cites the viewer's own unbroken attention
                 as the evidence for his claim. The viewer cannot disagree without scrolling —
                 and scrolling proves him right too. Unfalsifiable, self-referential, and it
                 arrives at the exact midpoint where attention decays.
               → It requires a self-demonstrating artifact to be true. **But the SENTENCE
                 SHAPE transfers to anything the reader is currently consuming** — including a
                 LinkedIn post. *"You've read this far, which is the whole point."*

OUTRO          "I wrote this whole pipeline from start to finish and I'm auto-improving it and
               releasing every single update for free. If you want videos similar to mine,
               just comment edit… **All right, Carl, I think we are ending this video now.**"
               verdict: **HIGH NOTE, then gate, then a signature.**
               → "Carl" is what he calls the AI. **He ends by giving the editor an instruction,
                 on camera.** NAME IT: **THE ARTIFACT ADDRESSED.** The demonstration continues
                 past the content. Appears in #5 too ("Carl, we're cutting this in 3, 2, 1").

NATIVE EMBED   ✅ **NATIVE.** The pain raised is "people post the first thing it spits out
               because they lack guidelines." The offer is the guidelines. **Second clean
               native embed in fifteen scripts** — the other is `projectonepercent01` #3.

E vs R         **BEAT, and beaten repeatedly** — at the itemised line 1, at the performed
               belief, at the reassignment, and at "that's exactly why you're still watching."
               **Four separate beats in one 200-word script.** No other script has more than one.

WHAT TO STEAL  1. **THE PRE-EMPTIVE OBJECTION IN POSITION TWO.** Say the criticism out loud
                  before the reader forms it, and stake something on being wrong.
               2. **THE PROOF IS YOU.** Use the reader's current attention as evidence.
               3. **REASSIGN THE BLAME** instead of reversing the verdict. "It's not the tool,
                  it's that nobody writes the guidelines." Stronger and less arguable.
               4. **PERFORM THE BELIEF IN FRAGMENTS.** "Purple. Gradient. Ugly stuff." Three
                  nouns beat a sentence of description.
WHAT TO DROP   Nothing structural.
```
