**Why he was in the top 3:** the tightest cluster on the entire watchlist — **17.2x / 13.6x /

not luck.

## ── STRUCTURES ──

### M1 · THE FREE-VERSION OF A PAID THING — 3 of 3 winners, 5 of 9 scored · **PROMOTE**

Every winner is the same sentence with the nouns swapped: *here is the expensive thing, here is
how to get it for nothing.*

| # | the promise | ratio |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | free ChatGPT plan → agentic coding | **17.19x** |
| 8 | **1 billion free tokens** in Claude Code | **13.59x** |
| 10 | a 200k-star educator released his skills free | **6.80x** |
| 12 | *"this unreleased tool lets you make AI systems for free"* | 1.00x |
| 5 | *"how to scrape any platform with AI"* | 0.22x |

```
0-3s   THE IMPOSSIBLE OFFER, stated as already true. No setup, no greeting.
       "देख रहा हूं तुमको क्लॉड कोड में फ्री के एक बिलियन टोकन दिलाता हूं"
       ("watch, I'm getting you a billion free tokens in Claude Code")
3-8s   "देखो सबसे पहले…" — straight into step one. NO context paragraph at all.
8-25s  Steps, narrated over the screen doing them. Three or four, never more.
close  Identical every time: "अगर पूरी गाइड चाहिए तो गाइड कॉमेंट करो, डायरेक्टली
       डीएम में लिंक आ जाएगा। थैंक्स फॉर वॉचिंग, टाटा बाय बाय।"
```

**RETENTION** — **5s:** the offer is not credible yet, so the viewer stays to catch the catch.
**12s:** the screen is already executing — proof arrives before doubt matures. **20s:** the step
count is small and visible, so the end is always near. **He never exceeds four steps.**
**REWARD** — a concrete saving, in money. The most legible reward class there is.
**MECHANIC** — orienting response via a violated price expectation. `psychology.md`: *reality
beats expectations → they stay.* A billion tokens for free is a large violation.
**AWARENESS** — **practitioner-level**, and it works anyway — because the *cost* is broad even
when the tool is not. "Free" needs no context.
**READER** — **peer.**
**DOWNGRADE** — survives fully. **Finding and handing over a free alternative requires zero
authority** — it is `niche.md` ring 1, *"resources I found and where"*, which is already
scheduled three per seven. **This is the shape ring 1 was waiting for.**
**TRANSFERS** — **yes.**

> **The catch, stated honestly:** #12 and #5 use the same shape at 1.00x and 0.22x. **The shape
> is necessary, not sufficient — the SIZE of the saving is what moves it.** "A billion tokens"
> and "the free ChatGPT plan" are big, specific, checkable numbers. *"How to scrape any platform"*
> is not a number at all. **Never run this shape without a figure in the first sentence.**

### M2 · NO INTRO, NO OUTRO, NO CONTEXT — 3 of 3 winners · **PROMOTE**

There is no *"hey guys"*, no *"in this video"*, no *"before we start"*. **The first spoken word is
already the offer**, and the last is a stock sign-off he never varies.

Post 8, in full, is **under 60 seconds and roughly 90 words.** Post 10 is similar.

**MECHANIC** — Speed, from `psychology.md`'s 4 S's. The whole first beat lands in under 8 words.
**RETENTION** — nothing to skip past, so nothing gets skipped past.
**DOWNGRADE** — survives. **This is a discipline, not a resource.**
**TRANSFERS** — **yes, and it is free.** Shubham's drafting instinct will be to explain first.
**This shape says: delete the first sentence, then delete the next one.**

---

### M3 · THE FIXED CLOSE — 9 of 9 scored · clears count, **fails transfer**

The same sign-off, word for word, on every post: *comment the keyword → link in DM → thank you for
watching → ta-da bye bye.*

**The repetition is the point** — a recognisable ending is a small brand asset, and regular
viewers know the deal without being told.

**TRANSFERS — the CTA half, no** (empty room, same as every other account here). **The
fixed-signature half, yes and cheaply** — a consistent closing line costs nothing and starts
compounding from post one. **Recorded as a habit to adopt, not a CTA to copy.**

### M4 · CAPTIONS AS LABELS, NOT COPY — 9 of 9

Every caption is a **title**, not a paragraph:

> *"How to use your free ChatGPT plan to do agent coding"* · *"How to get 1 billion tokens for
> free"* · *"Beat AI Skills"* · *"Instead of wasting your AI tokens, do this"*

**Zero hashtags. Zero emoji. No CTA in the caption at all** — it lives entirely in the audio.

> **This is the exact opposite of `_roshnichellani` and `socialmasla`, and it is worth flagging
> as a live conflict rather than resolving it here.** He runs 3-8 word captions and hits 17x.
> She runs 150-word captions with a question and hits 4.7x. **The watchlist does not agree on
> what a caption is for.** See CONFLICTS below.

## ── HITS vs MISSES ──

**Winners share:** a **number or a named quantity** in the first sentence — *one billion*, *free
plan*, *200,000 stars*.

**Misses share:** the same shape with the number removed.
- #5, **0.22x** — *"How to scrape any platform with AI"* — capability, no figure.
- #6, **0.26x** — *"1500 users in less than 10 days"* — a figure, but it is **his** result, not
  the viewer's saving. **A number about you is not a number about them.**
- #4, **0.26x** — *"Instead of wasting your AI tokens, do this"* — a warning, not an offer.

> **The rule this produces: the first sentence must contain a quantity the VIEWER gets.** Not a
> capability, not an achievement of yours, not a caution. A number they can have.

## ── CONFLICT WITH ANOTHER CREATOR — record, do not average ──

> **CAPTION LENGTH.** `developer_mannjadwani` runs 3-8 word titles, no hashtags, no CTA in the
> caption, and hits **17.19x**. `_roshnichellani` runs 100-150 word captions with a full argument,
> a question and five hashtags, and hits **4.69x**. `socialmasla` runs one line plus hashtags.
>
> **Do not average these into "keep captions medium".** They are different bets: he puts 100% of
> the load on the audio; she uses the caption as a second, silent version of the piece. **Both
> work. The choice is a test to run, not a rule to derive.**
