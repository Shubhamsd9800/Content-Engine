## 5 · HOW TO CHECK THIS WORK

**Against the material** — every claim in a teardown cites a post number and a ratio. Open
`work/creators/<slug>/raw/pieces.md`, find the post, check the number. If a claim has no post
number attached, it is an opinion and should be treated as one.

**Against the engine** — every promoted shape carries `RETENTION`, `REWARD`, `AWARENESS`,
`READER` and a `DOWNGRADE` line. `swipe.md` refuses an entry missing any of them. Scan for a
blank field; a blank field is a bug.

**Against `niche.md`** — every shape carries a RING and its cap. Sum the rings of a planned
week; if it is not 3-2-2, the plan is wrong even if every individual piece is good.

**Against reality** — nothing here has been tested. **Zero posts published.** Every promotion
is a hypothesis derived from someone else's account. The first twenty pieces are the experiment;
`swipe.md`'s own header says revise after 20.

> **The single most dangerous thing in this file is how confident it reads.** Six teardowns of
> other people's work is not evidence about Shubham's audience, because Shubham has no audience.
> **Treat every entry as a bet with a stated reason, not as a finding.**

## 7 · CORRECTION LOG

### C1 · S07's ANGLE RULE was over-narrow — Shubham's push-back, Day 26. **He was right.**

The Day 26 write-up presented *"the stage you are standing on right now"* as **the** angle. It is
**one** angle. `niche.md` says only that the angle must be **the reader's problem** — and a
reader's problem can be a decision they face, a belief the story corrects, a mechanism they can
reuse, or a real cost they can weigh. **Not only a stage-matching analogy.**

**A second error inside the first:** the corrected example opened *on the reader* — *"Physics
Wallah spent N years at the stage you are at right now."* **`_roshnichellani`'s own best post
does not do that.** It opens on an impossible fact about DNA. The reader-relevance arrives at the
**close**. **Open on the story. Land on the reader.** Opening on the reader is a lecture.

**What still stands:** *"X struggled and it's inspiring"* fails — not for missing one device, but
for having **no takeaway at all**. That is `niche.md` NOT-list item 5 and it is unchanged.

S07's ANGLE RULE in `swipe.md` rewritten accordingly.

### C2 · TWO BLOCKERS WITHDRAWN — Shubham, Day 26. **Both were mine, both were wrong.**

**S07 "research-heavy" — WITHDRAWN.** Ring 4 caps it at two per seven, so it never runs more
than twice a week regardless. **A cost that only lands twice a week is not a blocker, it is a
schedule.** The cap already solved the problem the blocker was worried about.

**S01 "nothing shipped to point at" — WITHDRAWN.** The blocker assumed S01 needs a *finished,

> **⚠️ ONE REAL RISK REPLACES IT, and it is not the same blocker.** The Content Engine is a
