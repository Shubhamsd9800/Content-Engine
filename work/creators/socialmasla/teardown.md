**Why he was worth reading:** the most **visibly templated** account on the watchlist. His

## ── STRUCTURES ──

### SM1 · THE PRICE-TAG KILL — 4 of 9 scored, 2 of 3 winners · **PROMOTE**

A single reusable sentence with two variables: **₹amount** and **thing**.

| # | caption | ratio |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | 🚨 Stop Wasting **₹1 Lakh** on **AI Agency Courses** | **4.25x** |
| 8 | Stop Wasting **₹15,000** on **Meta Ads Courses** | **4.15x** |
| 6 | Stop Wasting **₹15,000** on **YouTube Marketing Courses** | 1.00x |
| 4 | Stop paying **₹20,000** for **Social Media Marketing courses** | 0.78x |

```
0-3s   THE ANGER, then the number.
       "कौन से डॉलर बरस रहे हैं? ये AI एजेंसी के कोर्सेस 50,000 रुपए के क्यों खरीद रहे हो?"
       ("what, is it raining dollars? why are you buying these ₹50,000 agency courses?")
3-5s   THE FIXED PIVOT — same four words every time.
       "फ्री का रिसोर्स दे रहा हूँ, चेक इट आउट."   ("I'm giving you the free resource, check it out")
5-25s  THE INVENTORY. Rapid-fire list of everything the free thing covers.
       "live client build-outs, onboarding, service delivery, winning offer, ads,
        client acquisition, retention, sales calls, agency scaling — सब कुछ covered है"
20-30s THIRD-PARTY PROOF. Not his claim — other people's comments.
       "कॉमेंट में लोग literally बोल रहे हैं कि वीडियो देखकर उन्होंने अपना पहला client close कर दिया"
close  Comment → link.
```

**RETENTION**
- **5s** — money is at stake and he is angry about it *on the viewer's behalf.* The villain is
  named in the first sentence.
- **12s** — the **inventory**. Each item is a fraction of a second and there are twenty of them.
  Somewhere in the list is the one thing the viewer personally needs; they cannot leave until
  they hear it or the list ends. **The list length IS the retention mechanism.**
- **20s** — proof arrives, and it is not his. *"People in the comments are literally saying they
  closed their first client."*

**REWARD** — money not spent, plus a concrete resource. **The strongest reward class for an
Indian audience on this list, and it is not close.**
**MECHANIC** — `psychology.md`'s **villain rule**: *every strong position is against something —
not a person, a practice.* The villain here is the overpriced course, and it is the same villain
every time. **Consistency of villain is what makes an account feel like it stands for something.**
**AWARENESS** — **broad.** A price is universally legible.
**READER** — **peer**, decided at the hook end: it opens on *"what you are about to waste"*, which
is a builder's problem, not a business outcome.
**DOWNGRADE** — **survives completely, and it is the second shape that needs no authority at all.**
He is not the expert; **he is the person who found the free thing.** That is exactly `niche.md`'s
stated position — *I go a step ahead and bring back the part that works* — and it is ring 1.
**TRANSFERS** — **yes.**

> **The honest limit: 4 of 9 use it and the results are 4.25x / 4.15x / 1.00x / 0.78x.** Half hit,
> half did not. **The template alone does not carry a post.** What separates them:
>
> | | ₹ amount | result |
> |---|---|---|
> | winners | ₹1 lakh · ₹15,000 with a **12-hour** course behind it | 4.2x |
> | misses | ₹15,000 · ₹20,000, generic category, no course size named | ~1x |
>
> **The size of the number and the specificity of the replacement are the variables.** A vague
> saving is not a saving.

### SM2 · THE INVENTORY DUMP — 3 of 3 winners · **PROMOTE as a body pattern**

Where other creators explain one thing well, he **lists twenty things fast.**

> *"Instagram Ads covered है, Facebook Ads covered है, WhatsApp covered है, Threads covered है,
> Messenger covered है, Google Analytics तक covered है, Google Tag Manager तक covered है, Lead
> Generation campaign हो गए, Shopping campaign हो गए, App Install campaign हो गए — भाई सब कुछ
> covered है"*

**MECHANIC** — not persuasion. **Coverage anxiety.** The viewer is scanning for *their* item, and
the scan is the retention. The word *covered* repeats eight times; the repetition is the rhythm.
**RETENTION at 12s** — this is his whole middle section, and it works because no single item needs
to land. **Any one hit holds the viewer.**
**AWARENESS** — broad.
**DOWNGRADE** — survives. **Listing what a free resource contains requires no standing.**
**TRANSFERS** — **yes**, with one warning: it only works when the inventory is **real and long.**
Six items is not an inventory, it is a list, and the anxiety does not fire.

---

### SM3 · THIRD-PARTY PROOF — 2 of 3 winners · below bar · **WATCH — high value**

He never says *this is good.* He says **other people said it is good**, and points at where.

> *"अगर तुम्हें लग रहा है यार, मैं कुछ फेंक रहा हूँ यहाँ पे, तो बंदे का यहाँ पे review देखो"*
> — *"if you think I'm just talking nonsense here, look at this guy's review"*

Objection handling, which `psychology.md` rates the **highest-value hook modifier available** —
and here it is used mid-body rather than at the hook, at exactly the moment scepticism peaks.

**DOWNGRADE — this is the single most important adaptation in this teardown.** Shubham has **no
testimonials and no clients.** But the proof he points at is **not his own** — it is a stranger's
comment under someone else's video. **That is available to him today.** *"I didn't believe it
either — then I read the issues thread"* is the same move with zero track record.
**TRANSFERS** — **yes.** 2 of 3 keeps it below the bar; **flagged as the strongest WATCH in the
batch.**

in the distribution layer; not a content mechanic.

### SM5 · THE CAROUSEL EXCEPTION — post #9

Post #9 (3.33x) is a **carousel with no audio**. Shubham's file records only *"it is a carousel
image post, added in frames images."*

> **It is NOT analysed here and it must not be averaged with the two reels.** A carousel teaches
> layout and copy; it can teach nothing about retention or pacing. Its caption is a direct address
> to Meta — *"Dear Meta, advertisers don't need 50 new AI features every month"* — **a completely
> different register from everything else on the account**, and worth a separate look once its
> frames are read.

## ── HOOK PATTERNS ──

| Template | From |
|---|---|
| *"Stop wasting **₹<big number>** on **<category> courses**."* | #7 #8 #6 #4 |
| *"<Indignant question>? <Category> क्यों खरीद रहे हो? फ्री का रिसोर्स दे रहा हूँ."* | #7 |
| *"<Platform> just changed how <thing> works."* | #5 control |

**The template is the account.** He is not writing hooks; **he is filling in a form** — and it
produces 4x twice in one month.

---

## ── PACING ──

Voiceover over screen recordings of the free resource. Fast, no dead air. **Frames confirm large
Hindi/English on-screen text.** The rhythm is set by the list, not by cuts.

---

## ── LANGUAGE ──

**Hinglish, agency register.** Marketing nouns stay English (*Meta Ads, Lead Generation, Shopping
campaign, RTO*); everything structural is Hindi. Second person *तुम*, plus *भाई* — **more
familiar than any other account here.**

> *"भाई सब कुछ covered है"* — this register is a real asset. It is also the register Shubham
> noted `devtalksbusiness` for. **Two accounts independently landing on Indian raw-talk register
> is a signal worth carrying into `voice.md`.**

---

## ── HITS vs MISSES ──

**Winners share:** a **specific ₹ number** and a **named, sized free replacement** (*a 20-hour
course*, *a 12-hour course*).

**Misses share:**
- #11, **0.23x** — *"Your RTO problem isn't what you think"* — practitioner-level, no number, no
  free thing. **His worst post.**
- #5, **0.71x** — *"Meta just changed how ads can start conversations"* — pure news, nothing given.
- #12, **1.04x** / #10, **0.84x** — problem-framing posts with no resource attached.

> **Identical to `jasoncooperson`'s finding, from a completely different niche and language:
> news alone does nothing; news plus a thing you can take away performs.** Two independent
> accounts, same result. **This is now a cross-creator pattern, not a per-creator quirk.**

## ── DOES NOT TRANSFER ──

| | Why |
|---|---|
| Comment-to-DM · 🚨 · hashtag blocks | Distribution layer. |
| **Recommending free courses as the whole offer** | It positions him as a *curator*. `niche.md` wants **builder-who-also-finds**. The resource shape is ring 1 at three per seven — **a whole account of it is not the position.** |
| The agency/marketing subject | Not the niche. **Take the form, leave the topic** — the founding rule. |

## ── COVERAGE NOTE ──

**Adds:** the **template mindset** — proof that a repeatable fill-in-the-blank hook can be run
weekly without inventing something new each time. For someone with limited hours alongside a
full-time job, **this is arguably the most practically valuable finding in the batch.**

**Also adds:** third-party proof as a substitute for personal credibility.

**Still missing, after six teardowns:** a buyer-facing account with story or craft. Still absent.
