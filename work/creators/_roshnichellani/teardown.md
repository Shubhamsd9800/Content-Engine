## ── STRUCTURES ──

### R1 · THE CORRECTED ASSUMPTION — 6 of 11 explicit, 8 of 11 counting reveals · **PROMOTE**

She almost never opens on a fact. She opens on **a belief the viewer already holds, then breaks
it.** `psychology.md` calls this the contrarian intro and fixes the order: state the belief
first, *then* contradict — because the contrast has nothing to stand on otherwise.

```
0-3s   THE BELIEF, said as if agreeing.       "If Claude agrees with everything you say…"
       or THE WRONG GUESS, supplied for them. "You probably guessed Sundar Pichai. Maybe
                                               Satya Nadella."
3-8s   THE BREAK.                              "…you're using it wrong."  /  "You'd be wrong."
8-…    The real answer, explained from zero.
close  A question she actually wants answered.
```

**The counted openers:**

| # | opener | ratio |
|---|---|---|
| 4/6 | "If Claude agrees with everything you say, might as well you're using it wrong" | 4.10x / 1.06x |
| 3 | "You probably guessed Sundar Pichai. Maybe Satya Nadella. **You'd be wrong.**" | 1.00x |
| 9 | "one machine worth $380 MILLION. **Not NVIDIA. Not OpenAI. Not even the—**" | 0.64x |
| 5 | "ChatGPT isn't always lying… **sometimes it just doesn't know**" | 0.33x |
| 2 | "If you're learning AI in 2026, **don't stop at prompting**" | 0.72x |
| 7 | "**What's really happening** behind the AI boom?" | 0.20x |

**RETENTION** — the break creates a debt: she has taken something away and owes a replacement.
The viewer stays to be paid.
**REWARD** — a corrected model of something they thought they understood. **This is the highest-
value reward class in the whole watchlist** — not a tool, not a link, an actual belief change.
**MECHANIC** — contrarian intro / authentic polarization.
**AWARENESS** — **broad.** Every one of these is readable by someone with no context.
**READER** — **peer.**
**DOWNGRADE** — survives completely. It requires no authority: **you are not claiming to be
right, you are reporting that a common belief is wrong.** That is the cheapest credible position
available to someone 1.5 years in.
**TRANSFERS** — **yes.**

> **Note honestly: the count does not predict performance.** Posts 5, 7 and 2 use the same
> opener and did 0.33x, 0.20x and 0.72x. **The shape is necessary, not sufficient.** What
> separates her winners is the *subject*, not the structure — and subject is hers, not yours.

### R2 · THE DEFERRED ANSWER — 2 of 3 winners · *transcript-limited* · **WATCH**

She names the answer, then explicitly refuses to give it yet.

> *"Her reason: not a problem, but a **risk appetite problem**. Now, what does that mean?
> **Before that, here's what most people don't know.**"*

The phrase *"before that"* is doing all the work. She has already told you the answer exists and
has a name; the loop is open and cannot close until she chooses. Post 12 runs the same move —
*"Let's exactly understand what really happened"*, then two paragraphs of context first.

**RETENTION at 12s and 20s — this is her actual retention device.** Not pacing, not cuts. **A
sentence that promises and withholds in the same breath.**
**MECHANIC** — Zeigarnik, inside one piece rather than across posts.
**TRANSFERS** — **yes, and it is free.** It is a sentence, not a production technique.
**Below the bar at 2 of 3 only because the control set has no transcripts.** Re-check when more
of her reels are transcribed — this is the one most likely to promote next.

### R3 · THE OPINION CLOSE — and the natural experiment inside it · **PROMOTE (as a rule, not a shape)**

She runs **two different closes**, and they produce completely different engagement shapes:

| post | close | likes | comments | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| #11 | *"What should India do to retain talent like Anagha? Share your comments below"* | **5,086** | **128** | 40 : 1 |
| #4 | *"Comment COUNCIL and I'll send it to you"* | 2,981 | 1,578 | 1.9 : 1 |

**This is the cleanest evidence in the entire watchlist that the comment-to-DM CTA is a
distribution mechanic, not a content one.** Same creator, same week, same production. Swap the
last sentence and the comment count moves 12x while the likes move 1.7x.

**What it means for the rankings:** `engagement = likes + comments`, so **a comment-to-DM post
and an opinion post are not measured on the same scale.** #11 is her best post on likes by a wide
margin and only ranks first because of them.

**DOWNGRADE / TRANSFERS** — **the opinion close is the one that transfers today.** It costs
nothing, needs no automation, and `psychology.md` is explicit that at zero followers the CTA
should drive **conversation**, from the reader's own experience. *"What should India do"* is
exactly that. **The DM close is for later; this one is for now.**

> saying hey, I'm just going to sell it off — and that's when I was like, oh, she's really

lists five ways in — location, actions, thoughts, emotions, dialogue — and this hits three.

**It is the only moment in three reels where she stops explaining and starts remembering.** It
also carries the piece's one honest emotion (*"I'm very proud of Anagha"*).

**Once in three is noise and it is recorded as noise.** But it is the exact device `forseth.ai`
had none of, and it is the reason this account was worth pulling forward.

### R5 · THE MECHANISM EXPLAINED FROM ZERO — 3 of 3 winners

DNA storage explained in four sentences: *"DNA has four letters, A T G C. Your photos and videos
are already just ones and zeros. Software converts this into four letters and a machine writes

She names the thing a beginner would Google, every time. `psychology.md`'s **curse of knowledge**

**AWARENESS — broad, held all the way through the body**, which is rare. Most creators open broad
and drift into practitioner language by 15s. She does not.
**TRANSFERS — yes**, and it is the habit most worth stealing wholesale.

## ── PACING ──

Talking-head to camera, b-roll and screen recordings cut over the top. **English and Hindi switch
mid-sentence** — sometimes mid-clause — and the switch is not decorative: the English carries the
technical spine, the Hindi carries the emotional and rhetorical beats. *"…deep tech needs years of
R&D before it has anything to show investors"* runs in English; *"तो अगर इंडिया में इतना टैलेंट है
तो अनगा सैंफ्रांसिस्को जा क्यों रही है"* carries the frustration.

**She does not pause the explanation to switch languages. The switch IS the emphasis.**

## ── LANGUAGE ──

Mixed English/Hindi in one sentence, both directions. Sentence length 8-20 words — **longer than

warm, never lecturing. First person throughout. **Zero jargon left undefined.**
