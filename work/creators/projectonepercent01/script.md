## ── THE TEMPLATE — 3 of 3, near-verbatim ──

**All three winners open with the identical four-move formula. This is scripted, not incidental.**

```
1  "भाई साहब"                        direct-address particle. One word. No greeting.
2  A RESULT, already achieved         "just one reel got us 5,000+ followers"
3  "काश हमें ये और भी पहले पता होता"   "I wish we'd known this sooner"   ← 3 of 3, near-verbatim
4  "तो चलो पूरा process बताती हूँ"     the handoff into the body
```

> **MOVE 3 IS THE ONE TO UNDERSTAND.** It converts a flex into a confession. Without it,
> *"we got 5,000 followers"* is a scoreboard. With it, the same sentence becomes *"and we were
> late to this"* — the speaker joins the viewer on the wrong side of the knowledge, one beat
> after claiming the win. **The result stops being a boast and becomes a receipt.**
>
> **This is the mechanism that breaks `R-B` in `swipe.md`.** R-B says a creator's own number
> fails (`developer_mannjadwani`, *"1500 users in 10 days"*, **0.26x**). It fails there because
> nothing follows it. Here the regret line follows it, and the number works. **The variable is
> the regret line, not whose number it is.**
