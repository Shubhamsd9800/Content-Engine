## ── ⚠️ R-B IS CONTRADICTED BY THIS ACCOUNT, AND THE FIX IS INTERESTING ──

`R-B · THE NUMBER MUST BE THEIRS, NOT YOURS` rests on one example:
*"1500 users in 10 days"* — `developer_mannjadwani`'s own win — **0.26x**.

**This account opens on its own numbers in all three winners and they are its best posts:**

| # | the opening claim | whose number | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| #1 | *"just one reel got us 5,000+ followers"* | **theirs** | 4.93x |
| #2 | *"we took this page from 100 followers to 10K in 20 days"* | **theirs** | 4.02x |
| #3 | *"what a website we found"* | nobody's | 3.60x |

**R-B as written predicts these should fail. They are the account's top three.**

**The distinction that actually holds, tested against both accounts:**

> **A creator's own number works when it is the RECEIPT FOR AN ARTIFACT being handed over in the
> same breath. It fails when it is the whole point.**
>
> *"1500 users in 10 days"* — and then what? Nothing follows it that the viewer can take.
> It is a scoreboard. **0.26x.**
>
> *"5,000 followers from one reel"* — and here is the prompt that did it, free.
> The number is not the claim; **it is the proof that the prompt works.** **4.93x.**
>
> **The number must be EVIDENCE FOR SOMETHING THE VIEWER RECEIVES.** Whose number it is turns out
> to be the wrong axis. What matters is whether anything is attached to it.

**This lines up exactly with the amendment `shashwat___agarwal` forced** — where a post with *no*
number (*"I turned Claude Code into a website agency"*) beat two posts with specs (646 languages,
60K stars). **Two accounts, arriving at the same correction from opposite directions.**

> **PROPOSED, NOT APPLIED.** `R-B` currently reads *"the number must be theirs, not yours."*
> It should read something closer to: **"the opening must name what the viewer RECEIVES. A number
> earns its place only as evidence for that — never as a scoreboard, whoever's it is."**
> **Shubham's call. R-B is a promoted cross-creator rule and a teardown does not rewrite one.**

## ── ⚠️ R-C IS ALSO WRONG, AND THIS ONE MATTERS MORE ──

`R-C` and the `DID NOT TRANSFER` entry both reject the comment gate on this reasoning:
*"at zero followers this is shouting in an empty room."*

**This account has 25 posts and 17.7K followers.** It claims 100 → 10K in 20 days. It grew from
approximately nothing, on gated posts, and the gate is the only mechanism it runs.

**So does the rejection survive? Yes — but the stated reason is wrong, and the corrected reason
is more useful.**

**Look at this account's own misses. Every one of them is gated too:**

| # | gate | likes | engagement ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| #7 | *"Comment **Strategist**"* | 175 | **0.22x** |
| #6 | *"follow us + join our broadcast channel"* | 227 | **0.10x** |
| #8 | *"Comment **Journey**"* | **3** | **0.18x** |
| #11 | *"Comment **Prompt**"* | 659 | 0.68x |
| #9 | *"Comment **Content Calendar**"* | 497 | 0.60x |

**Identical gate. Same account, same weeks, same audience. 0.10x to 4.93x.** The gate is
constant across the whole feed and the outcomes span **fifty-fold.**

> ### THEREFORE: THE GATE IS A CONVERSION MECHANIC, NOT A DISTRIBUTION MECHANIC.
>
> It converts reach that already happened into followers and DMs. **It cannot create reach.**
> When the reel travels, the gate harvests it. When the reel doesn't, the gate sits on 3 likes.
>
> **What made #3 travel was not the gate. It was the hook, the format and the algorithm.**
>
> **The corrected rule:** *the gate is worth adding only to a piece that is already travelling,
> and it is never the reason a piece travels.* For this account today — zero posts, no evidence
> of what travels — **there is nothing to harvest, so the rejection stands.** But it stands
> because **there is nothing to convert**, not because "nobody is listening." The distinction
> matters at post 30, when something does travel and the question comes back.

**This also disqualifies the account's apparent lesson.** *"Gates grew them from 100 to 10K"* is
survivor reasoning. The gates were on the failures too.

## ── LANGUAGE ──

**Hindi-dominant Hinglish, `भाई साहब` register, first-person plural — `हम`, `हमने`, never `मैं`.**

**This is the only "we" account on the watchlist.** Every other creator speaks as an individual.
The plural does specific work: it makes the result institutional (*"we grew this page"*) and it
makes the offer sound like a team's product rather than one person's file.

**It also does something Shubham should not copy.** #2, verbatim: *"हम तो tech background से हैं
भी नहीं"* — *"we're not even from a tech background."* **Anti-credential positioning.** It widens
the audience by removing the barrier — and it is the precise inverse of `niche.md`'s authority
leg, which is built on **proof from real artifacts**. **Do not adopt. Recorded because it is the
first account on the list to position on the absence of expertise, and it works for them.**
