## 2 · WHY NOTHING PROMOTED — one reason, four times

**All four creators' winners run on a comment-to-DM gate.** `swipe.md` rejected that device on
Day 26 across all six Batch 1 accounts. It is now **ten accounts**.

The batch produced something Batch 1 did not have: **the gate measured against clean controls.**

**Two gradients, both monotonic, both new:**

1. **The lower the friction of the ask, the higher the multiplier.** *"Comment anything"* beats

   payload in the project (111,165 comments) and the second-lowest ratio, because 549k likes
   outran it.

**Gradient 2 is the one that matters.** It is what proves the correction in §3.

### C1 · `R-C` — the reasoning is wrong, the conclusion survives

**`R-C` and the `DID NOT TRANSFER` entry both say:** *at zero followers a keyword CTA is
"shouting in an empty room."*

**`projectonepercent01` disproves the premise.** 25 posts, 17.7K followers, claims 100 → 10K in

**But look at its own gated failures — same gate, same account, same weeks:**

```
#7  "Comment Strategist"      175 likes   0.22x
#6  "follow + broadcast"      227 likes   0.10x
#8  "Comment Journey"           3 likes   0.18x
#11 "Comment Prompt"          659 likes   0.68x
#9  "Comment Content Calendar" 497 likes   0.60x
```

**Identical gate. 0.10x to 4.93x — a fiftyfold spread.**

> **THE CORRECTION: the gate is a CONVERSION mechanic, not a DISTRIBUTION mechanic.**
> It converts reach that already happened into follows and DMs. It cannot create reach.
> When the reel travels, the gate harvests it. When it doesn't, the gate sits on 3 likes.
>
> **The rejection still stands for today** — zero posts means nothing to convert. But it stands
> because there is nothing to harvest, **not** because nobody is listening. **That distinction
> becomes live at post 30**, when something does travel and the question returns.

**How to check it:** `projectonepercent01/raw/pieces.md`, control rows #6–#11. Every one gated.

## 7 · THE ENGINE DEFECT — raised three times in four teardowns

**A body-level or frame-level pattern cannot clear the 5-of-10 bar, ever, by construction.**

The control set is captions only, truncated at 100 chars. Only 3 winners have transcripts, and
frames exist for at most 2. **Any pattern living in the body or the visuals can be counted across
at most 3, and the bar is 5.** Flagged in `_BATCH-1-AUDIT.md` §4. **It blocked four real findings
this batch: K2, K3, K4, W1/W2, D1.**

**Three options:**
1. A **separate bar for body/production patterns** — e.g. 3 of 3 winners AND a second account.
2. Transcribe part of the control set. Expensive; A6 is manual.
3. Accept it: body patterns promote only as **cross-creator** findings, never as shapes.

**Option 3 is what the engine is already doing by accident.** Making it deliberate costs nothing
and would have let `K2`, `W1` and `D1` through as rules. **Shubham's call.**
