**IDEA 1 — the strongest thing in this queue, and it has one blocker.**
The story is set on an employer project, with an employer's leader. **NOT-list item 1.**
It does not need them: *"six or seven months ago I asked someone more senior than me…"* carries
the whole story with no company, no project, no role named. `ledger.md` line 83 already records
this exact workaround — **write it as "a senior architect I know."** The insight survives intact.
Shape: the corrected belief. Real cost, real timeline, and he is the one who was wrong — which
is why it does not read as preaching.

**IDEA 2 — ring 4, BUCKET = moving, and the commentary rule governs it.**
A screenshot plus a reaction is not a post. A screenshot plus **his read** is.
*"AGI is here"* and *"software engineers are dead"* are reactions; everyone already has one.
**The post is what changes for someone building on it.** Comparing what people actually
SHIPPED with each model is the version that passes.
⚠️ **VERIFY THE LAUNCH FIRST.** Claude cannot confirm this model release from training and must
search before a post is staked on it. Never publish an unverified product claim.

**RING BUDGET CHECK — ring 4 is TWO of every seven.** Idea 2 spends one of them. AI-news posts
are the easiest to write and the fastest way to drift into a pure-shareable run that reaches
peers and no buyers. See `strategy.md`.
