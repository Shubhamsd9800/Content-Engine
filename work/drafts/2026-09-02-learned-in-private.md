## THE POST — as it goes to LinkedIn

I wasted a month of my Claude subscription.

Last month I learned more than in any month of my career. From the outside it looks exactly like I did nothing.

There is a war room where I plan and brainstorm every day, the way a small company runs itself. Next to it, a content engine that plans and writes content, and it has never produced a post. I built a workflow for shipping applications and never built an application. I drafted proposals for two real businesses in my city and never sent either one.

All of that felt like progress, and it was. But building and learning can be done alone. Publishing cannot, and that is the one that kept moving to tomorrow.

I called it preparing. It was hiding, and hiding looks exactly like work.

I kept waiting to be ready. Ready is not a state a system reaches. It is permission, and I kept refusing to give it to myself.

That changes today.

The first draft of this post came out of the content engine I built. I rewrote it, because a human in the loop is not optional.

If you are learning every day and nobody outside can see any of it, you are not preparing either.

The whole system is on GitHub. Link is in the first comment, if you want to see what a month of building in private adds up to.
