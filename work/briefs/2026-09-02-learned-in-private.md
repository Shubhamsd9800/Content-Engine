```
         ← never "what you should do"

```

**Why the proof beat cannot be cut.** Without it, the post is a confession with nothing
under it — motivational content as a FORM, which is banned. With it, the confession earns
the right to show the work, and that is the only way to show work at zero followers without
it reading as a flex.

## THE LESSON — how it is phrased, and why

**Shubham's call: the post carries a lesson AND recognition. Both.**

**The risk, recorded:** at zero published posts, telling people what to do about publishing
is preaching from inside the problem. It is the pratfall trap — a failure post from someone
not yet established reads as incompetence unless the competence travels with it.

**The fix is grammatical, not structural:**

```
✗  "Here's what you should do."          preaching. he has not solved it.
✓  "Here's what I'm doing differently,
    starting with this post."             provable. the post is the evidence.
```

Same lesson lands. The reader still recognises themselves and still knows what to change.
**And it is defensible**, because the post in front of them is the proof that he did it.

   a contrast.

   were written and NOT sent — that is the point, and it must not read as client work.

6. **No money numbers.** No rates, no earnings, no cost-of-the-month framing in currency.

7. **The rejection/failure quote Shubham raised is OUT.** *"The people with the most
   rejections succeed"* is a quote with nothing under it — banned as a FORM. The story with
